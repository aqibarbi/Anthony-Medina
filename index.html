Methodology

  1. Explore

    Spend time where the problem lives: field research, surveys and interviews. Write down what people do, not what they say they do.

  2. Define

    Turn findings into one clear problem statement the whole team can agree on.

  3. Ideate

    Sketch many ideas fast, on paper first, then pick the few worth testing.

  4. Prototype

    Build low to high fidelity flows in Figma that are cheap to change.

  5. Test

    Put the prototype in front of real users, fix what breaks, then run it again.
